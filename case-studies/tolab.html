Tolab — University Students Hub App

A mobile platform tailored for university students — especially the Faculty of Computers and Information — centralizing academic files, schedules and interactive campus features.

RoleUI/UX Design, IA, Design System
TimelineOct 2025 – Dec 2025
PlatformMobile App (iOS/Android)
ToolsFigma, Auto Layout

Overview

Students juggle scattered file shares, group chats and printed schedules just to stay organized. Tolab set out to become the single place for academic life on campus.

The Challenge

The app needed a flexible color system that worked identically well in Light and Dark mode, plus an information architecture flexible enough for different departments' course structures.

Process

How the project moved from research to a production-ready system.

01

Campus research

Interviewed students to understand where academic information actually lives today, and where it breaks down.

02

Information architecture

Organized files, schedules and announcements into a structure that mirrors how students think about their semester.

03

Dual-mode UI system

Designed a flexible color system supporting both Light and Dark modes from the same token set.

04

Component handoff

Delivered a structured design system with organized component states for developer handoff.
