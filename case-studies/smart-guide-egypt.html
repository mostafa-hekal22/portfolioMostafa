Smart Guide Egypt — Tourist & Guide Mobile App

An innovative tourism app bridging tourists with certified tour guides across Egypt — with dual personas, interactive maps and AR-powered discovery.

RoleUI/UX Design, Dual-Persona System
TimelineDec 2025 – Jun 2026
PlatformMobile App
ToolsFigma, Prototyping

Overview

Smart Guide Egypt bridges travelers with certified local guides through two distinct experiences living in one app — Tourist Mode and Guide Mode.

The Challenge

Designing two full personas inside a single product meant every shared screen had to flex for two very different intents — discovery for tourists, management for guides — without duplicating the whole app.

Process

How the project moved from research to a production-ready system.

01

Dual-persona architecture

Designed separate Tourist and Guide modes, each with its own navigation and priorities.

02

Interactive discovery

Built smart destination discovery with interactive maps and AR-powered points of interest.

03

Guide dashboard

Designed booking and availability tools so certified guides can manage their schedule on the go.

04

Design system delivery

Delivered a complete design system, user flows and structured UI components for handoff.
