EduHub — Smart Educational Platform

A smart educational platform with four dedicated portals for Admins, Teachers, Students and Parents — designed from information architecture to production-ready UI.

RoleUI/UX Design, Design System
TimelineJan 2026 – May 2026
PlatformMobile App
ToolsFigma, Auto Layout

Overview

EduHub needed to serve four very different users — administrators, teachers, students and parents — inside one coherent platform, without any of them feeling lost in tools meant for someone else.

The Challenge

The core challenge was complexity management: each portal needed its own permissions, data views and workflows, while still feeling like one connected product with a shared visual language.

Process

How the project moved from research to a production-ready system.

01

Mapping the four portals

Defined distinct user journeys and permission boundaries for Admin, Teacher, Student and Parent roles before touching any screens.

02

Information architecture

Structured navigation and content hierarchy around what each role actually needs to do daily, not just what data exists.

03

Wireframes & flows

Built low-fidelity flows for enrollment, grading, scheduling and messaging, tested against real classroom scenarios.

04

Design system & handoff

Delivered a component library with documented states, ready for developer implementation.
