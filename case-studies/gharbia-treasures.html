Gharbia Treasures — Regional Industrial Web Portal

A specialized regional web portal highlighting local manufacturing, traditional crafts and economic reports for the Gharbia governorate.

RoleUI/UX & Web Design
TimelineMar 2025 – May 2025
PlatformWeb Portal
ToolsFigma

Overview

Gharbia's manufacturers and craftspeople had no unified digital presence. This portal needed to make dense, content-heavy information feel approachable and easy to browse.

The Challenge

Economic reports and industrial listings are naturally text-heavy — the challenge was building visual hierarchy and structured navigation without flattening it into a wall of text.

Process

How the project moved from research to a production-ready system.

01

Content audit

Reviewed the manufacturing, crafts and report content that the portal needed to organize.

02

Card-based architecture

Designed a card-based layout to break dense content into scannable, structured sections.

03

Navigation structure

Built clear, structured navigation so visitors could move between categories without friction.

04

Visual hierarchy pass

Refined type scale and spacing for a seamless, content-heavy reading experience.
