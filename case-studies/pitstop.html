PitStop — Car Maintenance Mobile App

A mobile application connecting car owners with trusted workshops — covering map browsing, booking, payments and live maintenance tracking.

RoleUI/UX Design, User Journey Mapping
TimelineJul 2025 – Sep 2025
PlatformMobile App
ToolsFigma, Prototyping

Overview

Finding a reliable workshop is usually a mix of guesswork and word of mouth. PitStop set out to replace that with a transparent, trackable booking experience.

The Challenge

The flow had to connect four very different moments — discovery, booking, payment and tracking — into one journey that felt as simple as ordering a ride.

Process

How the project moved from research to a production-ready system.

01

User journey mapping

Charted the full path from noticing a car issue to a completed, paid service visit.

02

Map-based discovery

Designed workshop browsing around location, ratings and trust signals.

03

Booking & payments

Built a streamlined booking flow with transparent pricing and secure payment steps.

04

Live tracking UI

Designed real-time status screens so owners always know where their car stands.
